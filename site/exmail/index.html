Tencent Exmail on a Mac or a Windows PC

Sign in with your address and password. Ragdoll Mail sees that your company's mail is on Tencent Exmail and fills in the servers itself.

Download for Mac Apple siliconDownload for Windows 10 and 11

Three steps

  1. Check that IMAP is on

    In Exmail on the web, open the settings and make sure the IMAP/SMTP service is on. If your company turned it off for everyone, ask whoever runs your mail.

  2. Make a client password if you need one

    If you sign in to Exmail with WeChat or a dynamic code, mail apps need a client password (客户端专用密码) instead of your web password. You make it in the account security part of the settings.

  3. Sign in to Ragdoll Mail

    Type your address and that password. The first time, it reads your mail into a local index; after that, search answers at once, also offline.

Server settings

For Ragdoll Mail these are filled in for you. They are the same in any other mail app.

Why Exmail's own search finds everything, or nothing

Exmail does not search inside mails when a mail app asks it to: it gives back the whole folder. On a real Exmail mailbox with 6,442 mails, a search returned 4,988 mails for any word, even for a word we made up.

Ragdoll Mail keeps its own full-text index on your computer. It returns only the mails with your words, in under a tenth of a second, in Chinese and English, also with no network.

Questions

It says the password is wrong. What now?

Most of the time Exmail wants the client password (客户端专用密码), not the one you use on the web. If that is not it, check that IMAP/SMTP is on in the settings.

Does it work with other company mail?

Yes. It also finds the servers itself for Alibaba Mail, NetEase enterprise mail, Google Workspace, Zoho and Fastmail. For any other IMAP mailbox, type the server names once.

Does my mail go to your servers?

No. The app talks only to your own mail server. The index and your password stay on your computer.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is free and open source (MIT).
