Where Ragdoll Mail is strong

Search. It keeps a full-text index on your computer, so a word, a name or an order number finds the right mails in under a tenth of a second, in Chinese and English, also offline. Mailboxes like Tencent Exmail give back the whole folder when a mail app asks them to search.

Privacy. The app talks only to your own mail server. Your password is in the system's password store.

Fewer mistakes. Undo send, send later, and a check for a missing attachment, an empty subject or a [Name] left in the text.

Chinese mail. Names and file names from older Chinese mail systems show as they should.

Where the others are better

Ragdoll Mail has no phone app and no calendar, and it shows one mailbox at a time. If you need those, use one of the others, or use both: they read the same mailbox.

Questions

Is there a good Foxmail alternative for Mac?

Ragdoll Mail is one: it runs on Mac (Apple silicon) and Windows, signs in to the same mailboxes as Foxmail (Tencent Exmail, QQ Mail, 163 and any IMAP mailbox), and its search answers at once.

Which mail app works best with Tencent Exmail?

Any IMAP app can read Exmail, but Exmail's own search gives back the whole folder. Ragdoll Mail searches its own index instead, so only the mails with your words come back.

Can I keep my current mail app?

Yes. Ragdoll Mail reads the same mailbox. Your phone and your other mail apps keep working as before.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is free and open source (MIT). No account, no ads.
